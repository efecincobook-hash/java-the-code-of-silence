Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Policy explains how the personal information you provide on this website is collected, used, stored, and protected. It applies to the website for the book "The Code of Silence" and to the sign-up form on it. The site is operated from Brazil, so our data practices follow Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Lei nº 13.709/2018, "LGPD"). Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under your local privacy laws.

Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent, which you give freely and knowingly by checking the consent box before submitting the form. Technical security records are also processed based on our legitimate interest in keeping the site safe.

How and where data is stored

Form submissions are saved to a Google Sheets spreadsheet through a Google Apps Script service, both operated by Google LLC. Only the site owner has access to the spreadsheet. Data is transmitted over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical access data, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with the service providers needed to run this website:

  • Google LLC — spreadsheet storage and the script that receives form submissions.
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting.
  • Digistore24 — our authorized reseller and payment processor. When you click a purchase button, you are taken to Digistore24's checkout, which has its own privacy policy and handles your order and payment details independently. We never receive or store your card details.

These providers may process data outside your country of residence, including in the United States and the European Union, under their own contractual and legal safeguards.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as you remain interested in the series or until you ask us to delete it. Sign-ups with no interaction for 24 months are deleted. Once you request deletion, your data is removed within 15 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

At any time, you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold data about you and give you access to it;
  • correct incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • anonymize, block, or delete unnecessary or excessive data;
  • transfer your data to another provider;
  • delete data processed based on your consent;
  • tell you who your data has been shared with;
  • withdraw your consent, at any time and free of charge.

How to make a request: email efecincobook@gmail.com with the subject Privacy request, including the email address you signed up with and what you'd like us to do. We reply within 15 days.

Cookies

This website does not use its own tracking cookies or advertising tools. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will update this Policy and show a cookie notice before any data is collected.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission, restricted access to the spreadsheet through an authenticated Google account, and origin checks on the service that receives form submissions. No system is completely immune to incidents. If a significant security incident occurs, affected users and the relevant authorities will be notified as required by law.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy at any time. The date of the latest update appears at the top of this page. If we make significant changes to how we use your data, we will let subscribers know by email.
